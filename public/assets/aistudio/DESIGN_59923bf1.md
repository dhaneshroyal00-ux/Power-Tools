## Brand & Style
This design system captures the dynamic, hyper-functional aesthetic of an authentic Indian rural machinery, service, and power tool enterprise rooted in Pichatur, Andhra Pradesh. Built for farmers, agricultural contractors, local mechanics, civil masons, and borewell technicians, the visual posture avoids sterile corporate minimalism in favor of vibrant, high-contrast, utility-first clarity inspired by physical storefront signboards, flex banners, and heavy-duty machinery nameplates.

The style blends modern industrial clarity with high-contrast commercial signage elements:
- **High-contrast, action-driven hierarchy:** Key actions like WhatsApp inquiry, rental price checks, phone calls to the store proprietor, and equipment availability statuses are unmistakably prominent.
- **Bilingual functional framing:** Layouts are engineered to naturally house English alongside regional Telugu labels (`అద్దెకు`, `సేల్స్ & సర్వీస్`), reflecting authentic on-the-ground commerce.
- **Physical storefront cues:** Sturdy structural borders, bold pricing badges, caution-striped accents, and tactile utility tags reminiscent of heavy equipment labels, agricultural sprayers, and power generators.

## Colors
The color palette directly references the visual environment of power machinery, commercial flex boards, and warning tags:
- **Primary (`#E5252A` - Vivid Warning Red):** Used for immediate visual impact—machinery alerts, primary action buttons, rental price highlights, and critical commercial triggers.
- **Secondary (`#0D55D8` - Electric Cobalt Blue):** Anchors trust, enterprise service banners, category badges, technical warranty marks, and navigation bars.
- **Tertiary (`#FFB800` - Energetic Marigold Yellow):** Functions as high-visibility caution yellow for badges, availability chips, tool power ratings, and special rental day-rate tags.
- **Neutral (`#141923` - Crisp Industrial Slate):** Forms high-contrast headings, borders, and dark utility cards, grounding bright colors against crisp white canvas surfaces (`#FFFFFF` and `#F5F7FA`).
- **Functional WhatsApp Accent (`#25D366`):** Dedicated exclusively for one-tap WhatsApp inquiries and real-time inventory quotes directly to the shop owner.

## Typography
The typography leverages Space Grotesk for sharp, mechanical display headers and heavy-duty utility labels, paired with Chivo for readable, dense body copy and technical specifications (such as HP ratings, blade sizes, fuel capacity, and rental terms).

- Display and headline levels emphasize uppercase or robust bold weights to mimic mechanical signage, flex boards, and machinery plaques.
- Numeric elements (prices, phone numbers, horsepower figures, and GSTIN indicators) are set in Space Grotesk for distinct tabular clarity.
- Regional bilingual labels (Telugu translations) inherit line-height padding multipliers of 1.2x to prevent glyph clipping while preserving baseline alignment.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column layout handles inventory listings, service booking sections, and equipment catalog cards across screen sizes:
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4 columns with `1rem` margin and `1rem` gutter. Full-width sticky bottom bars for rapid direct dial and WhatsApp contact.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters for 2-column machine cards.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12 columns bounded at a max-width of `1280px` with `2rem` outer margin and `1.5rem` gutter, accommodating 3 or 4 catalog items per row.

Spacing between components follows a strict industrial density principle: compact enough to present dense inventory and immediate pricing without excessive scroll, yet spacious enough for easy touch targets during outdoor usage under bright sunlight.

## Elevation & Depth
Rather than relying on vague, diffuse shadows that wash out under bright outdoor glare, this system uses crisp, low-blur tactile elevation paired with mechanical borders:
- **Surface Layer 0 (Canvas):** Crisp chalk white (`#FFFFFF`) or off-white industrial gray (`#F5F7FA`).
- **Surface Layer 1 (Card & Containers):** Pure white with a 1.5px solid border in Slate-200 (`#E2E8F0`) and a crisp drop shadow (`0 2px 0 #141923` or `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(20, 25, 35, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Layer 2 (Active & Sticky Modules):** 2px solid Dark Slate (`#141923`) border with a 4px hard offset shadow (`4px 4px 0px #141923`), imparting a physical machinery tag quality.
- **Modal / Floating Actions:** High-contrast solid backings with bold borders, guaranteeing instant legibility on low-cost mobile displays outdoors.

## Shapes
A "Soft" roundedness level (`roundedness: 1` = 0.25rem base radius) is specified throughout the design system. 
- Component containers, machine listing cards, and input fields utilize tight `4px` (`0.25rem`) corners to retain an authentic mechanical, boxed-hardware silhouette.
- Badges and status pills use maximum `8px` (`0.5rem` - `rounded-lg`) to balance utilitarian structure with approachable legibility.
- Pill shapes are strictly avoided for core components, preserving the sturdy, industrial aesthetic of agricultural machinery, power tools, and metal hardware.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Call Store / Book Machine):** Background in Vivid Warning Red (`#E5252A`), white text, 1.5px border in `#B71216`, uppercase Space Grotesk Bold, 4px border radius. 
- **WhatsApp Direct Connect:** Background in WhatsApp Emerald (`#25D366`), white bold text, paired with WhatsApp icon. Features a 1px solid `#1EBE5D` border.
- **Secondary / Rental Check:** Background in Electric Cobalt Blue (`#0D55D8`), white text, crisp 4px radius.
- **Tertiary / Filter Buttons:** Background in pure white with a 1.5px solid `#141923` border and dark slate text.

### Machinery & Rental Cards
- Built with a white surface, a 1.5px `#E2E8F0` border, and a 4px corner radius.
- Contains a dedicated top banner showing the machinery category (e.g., "CHAINSAW / చైన్సా", "EARTH AUGER / ఎర్త్ ఆగర్").
- Distinct dual-pricing badge: **Daily Rental (₹ / Day)** highlighted in Marigold Yellow (`#FFB800`) box, alongside **Outright Sale (MRP / Offer Price)** in bold Warning Red.
- Status tag indicates live store availability: `"IN STOCK / అందుబాటులో ఉంది"` (green outline) or `"CURRENTLY ON RENTAL"` (amber badge).

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Category Chips:** Electric Cobalt Blue subtle tint background with solid blue text and 1px border.
- **Power Rating & Fuel Badges:** Dark slate background with high-contrast yellow/white text (e.g., `58CC 2-STROKE`, `3.0 HP`, `220V ELECTRIC`).
- **GST / Official Verification:** Small mono-spaced tag carrying verified GSTIN badge for agricultural subsidy validation.

### Input Fields
- White background, 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border, 4px radius, with labels rendered in dark slate Space Grotesk.
- Focused state features a crisp 2px border in Electric Cobalt Blue (`#0D55D8`) without blurry focus rings.

### Lists & Service Rate Cards
- High-contrast alternating row stripes (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`).
- Monospaced numeric tables for blade sharpening charges, oil refills, motor rewinding rates, and consumable replacement pricing.
